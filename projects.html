Featured Case Study

Amazon Reforestation Corridor

Restoring a critical 12,000-hectare rainforest corridor in the Amazon basin, reconnecting fragmented habitats for endangered species.

12K Hectares
2.4M Trees
85+ Species

In the heart of the Amazon basin, a 12,000-hectare corridor is being restored to reconnect fragmented rainforest — creating safe passage for endangered species and a sustainable future for indigenous communities.

2.4M Trees planted
300 Indigenous stewards
85+ Species protected
  • Acoustic sensors monitor wildlife activity 24/7 across 150 listening stations
  • Indigenous communities earn sustainable income through agroforestry cooperatives
  • Carbon capture equivalent to removing 45,000 cars from roads annually
  • Over 1,200 hectares of degraded cattle pasture reforested since 2021

Project Timeline

2021 Baseline survey & community agreements
2022 First 500,000 trees planted
2024 1M trees milestone reached
2026 Full corridor completion target
